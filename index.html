Critique of the Collection: This bibliography is an absolute masterclass in mapping comparative digital policy (India/Sri Lanka) to Western political theory (Hobbesian State of Nature). However, considering the audience is the 'Peradeniya Philosophy Day 2025', it completely lacks a South Asian ontological framework. The Surprising Synthesis (Your Intellectual Trump Card):

Do not just stop at Hobbes. Contrast the Algorithmic Leviathan with the Buddhist concept of the Aggañña Sutta (The discourse on the genesis of society). In Buddhist political philosophy, the state did not emerge from a fearful Leviathan, but from the Mahasammata (The Great Elect)—a ruler chosen by the people strictly to maintain order and bound by Dharma (ethics and duty).

Your surprising argument for the conference: The Algorithmic State in South Asia (like Aadhaar and SL's PDPA) initially promised to be a digital Mahasammata (a trusted, duty-bound system to distribute welfare efficiently). However, without democratic Dharma (oversight), it is mutating into a Hobbesian Leviathan—coercive, untrustable, and absolute. Bridging Western digital authoritarianism with foundational Indo-Sri Lankan philosophy will give you a profound, standing-ovation-worthy conclusion.
